01 The one idea

Accuracy, free, and public normally fight each other: good speech recognition costs money per hour, and a free tool's bill grows with every user. Running Whisper on the user's own GPU settles all three at once.

Their machine — all heavy work
  • Tab audio + microphone capture
  • Voice detection, then Whisper on WebGPU
  • Caption and active-speaker reading
  • Fusion and glossary correction
Your server — text only
  • Meeting records and transcripts
  • Summarising into minutes
  • Editing, exports, action items
  • Roughly 60 KB per meeting

Audio never crosses that dashed line. That keeps cost at zero however many people use it, and makes your audio never leaves your computer a true claim — the strongest thing a recording tool can say.

02 How the accuracy is won

Meet hands you two broken views of the same conversation. Captions know exactly who spoke but garble the words. Whisper gets the words right but cannot tell people apart. Each fixes the other's weakness.

This gives named speakers with no diarisation vendor, which is normally the most expensive line on a transcription bill. Two supports make it hold up: voice detection before Whisper, because Whisper invents sentences during silence, and feeding it the participant names and your project words up front, which is what turns "geodesic" into "Geodesis".

04 What the user does

The first run carries all the setup. Every meeting after it asks for nothing at all.

First install once, ~2 min

  1. Add to Chrome.
  2. Sign in with Google — one click.
  3. Minuto measures the GPU, picks a model, downloads it in the background.
  4. Told plainly: high accuracy ready shortly, your first meeting is captured either way.

Every meeting after zero clicks

  1. Open any Meet link. Minuto turns itself on.
  2. It says once in the chat that it is taking notes, and switches captions on itself.
  3. You do nothing. Press Alt+S to mark a moment worth keeping.
  4. Call ends, it notices, and minutes arrive in under a minute.

Three choices carry "anyone can use it": turn captions on for them rather than telling them to, detect the end of the call rather than relying on a stop button, and never block on the model download — capture at whatever quality is ready, then quietly re-transcribe and upgrade the minutes once the better model lands.

05 Build order

Each phase ends in something that works. The risk is concentrated in the first one on purpose.

06 Settled

QuestionAnswerWhy it matters
Model size Whisper large-v3-turbo Your RTX 3050 runs it faster than realtime. Smaller models drop Hindi spans rather than degrading gently.
Release path GitHub and personal use first Keeps Calendar access optional, which keeps you off Google's slow verification queue until you choose otherwise.
Language Hinglish and Indian English Your own recordings become the main benchmark. Published Whisper scores are clean English and will flatter you.
Audio storage Never uploaded, discarded after use Zero storage cost, far less to go wrong, and a trust claim that is actually true.
Open, needs real recordings

Whisper takes a language hint, and for mixed Hindi-English there is no right answer on paper. English transliterates Hindi roughly; Hindi returns Devanagari, wrong for minutes people read in Latin script; automatic detection can flip mid-call and mix both scripts. Phase 0 runs all three over your own calls and picks on measured results.
